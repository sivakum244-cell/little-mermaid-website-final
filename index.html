The experience

      
Alleppey, seen from the water

      
Little Mermaid is a houseboat stay in Alleppey (Alappuzha), Kerala. Spend unhurried time on board, with lake and river views, open-air seating and a sun terrace on the top deck.

      
The listing describes comfortable family rooms with air conditioning and private bathrooms, a lounge and outdoor seating, free Wi-Fi, and onboard dining. The Superior Cabin on Boat also has its own fully equipped kitchen.

      
Canoeing and fishing are listed as activities. Canoeing has an additional charge, and charges may apply for fishing.

      
Cruise routes, durations, meal inclusions and whether the boat is private for your party are not listed. Ask the property when you enquire.
