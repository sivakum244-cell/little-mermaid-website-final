The experience

      
A relaxed stay on Alleppey's backwaters

    

    

      
Little Mermaid is a houseboat stay in Alleppey (Alappuzha), Kerala. Spend unhurried time on the water, with lake or river views from your balcony, outdoor seating to settle into, and a sun terrace for the open air.

      
The listing describes comfortable family rooms with air conditioning and private bathrooms, a lounge and outdoor seating area, free Wi-Fi, and onboard dining. The Superior Cabin on Boat also has its own fully equipped kitchen.

      
Canoeing and fishing are listed as activities and may be available for an additional charge. Please ask when you enquire.

      
Routes, cruise durations, meal inclusions and exclusivity are not stated here. Ask the property to confirm them for your dates.
